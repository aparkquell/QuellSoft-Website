## A practical starting point

The best healthcare AI projects start with a narrow task: summarize, classify, route, or extract. That keeps the project useful and easier to govern.

## What to avoid

- Vague "AI transformation" language
- Overly broad automation goals
- Interfaces that hide the source data

## A better pattern

Choose one workflow, define the guardrails, and make the human handoff explicit.
