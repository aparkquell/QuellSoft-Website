## Why modernization matters

If the site is the first proof point a buyer sees, the redesign has to improve clarity without breaking continuity.

## What to keep

- Existing URLs
- Search equity
- High-intent service pages

## What to improve

- Information hierarchy
- Page speed
- Stronger proof sections
- Better vertical framing
